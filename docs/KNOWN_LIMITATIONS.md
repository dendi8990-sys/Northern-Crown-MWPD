# Known limitations and claim boundaries

- Validation is scoped to the frozen v0.4-RC1 tests and targets; it is not universal cross-domain validation.
- `Z_R` measures candidate identity robustness, not physical truth.
- `T_env64` measures local environmental ridge-linearity/anisotropy transfer, not direct filament truth.
- Do not claim universal cosmic-web taxonomy or universal superiority over HDBSCAN or DisPerSE.
- Do not claim exact filament geometry recovery.
- Temporal material persistence is not established: `PERSIST01` is a frozen FAIL.
- Direct DisPerSE correspondence is not established as a campaign-level claim: `TRUTH03A` completed with 2/3 target PASS, but its preregistered campaign rule required 3/3, so the campaign is FAIL.
- Heavy formal validation datasets are not bundled with the source repository.
- The public CLI is a compact detector/reproducibility interface, not a repackaging of every historical Binder/Jupyter orchestration runner.
